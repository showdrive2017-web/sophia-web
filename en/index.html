FAQ

Is SOPHIA free?

The trial is free: up to 10 stars and 1 project, no sign-in needed. If you like it, a one-time purchase (no subscription) gives you stars without limit and up to 5 projects.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. There is no account and no sign-in, and your stars and notes are never sent to a server. You can export your work as text or as a project file.

How is it different from a mind map?

You don't fill in boxes. Double-tap to turn a thought into a star, then trace from star to star to connect them. “Summary” turns your connected stars into text, in order — a first draft for plans and scripts.

Is there a PC version?

Yes, for Windows 10 / 11 (free trial). Open what you exported from your phone on a big screen. Buying the full version on your phone unlocks the PC version too. Download it from the PC version page.

Is there an Android version?

The Android version is coming soon to Google Play.The Android version is on Google Play.

What happens when I change phones?

Export your work as a project file and import it on the new device. The full version can be restored with “Restore purchases” on the purchase screen.

Which languages does it support?

English, Spanish, French, German and Japanese. It follows your device's language.
